[ WHO WE ARE ]

Removing Uncertainty in Power.

The LightForce Mandate

LightForce Electric Limited is an independent consumer energy company committed to removing uncertainty in Nigeria’s power system. We provide quality electrical products, solar and energy solutions, and professional installations that help homes, businesses, and industries enjoy reliable power every day.

We operate an asset-light, technology-enabled model that allows us to deliver top-quality products and services efficiently, transparently, and affordably without warehouse inflation taxes.
